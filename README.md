## 💼 Professional Experience

**🧠 AI & Full Stack Engineer — Final-Year Project** @ I-Way | *February – July 2026*  
Designed and developed a digital twin platform for intelligent monitoring of an instrumented building, combining IoT data, AI, and interactive 3D visualization.

- Built a FastAPI backend with HTTP/MQTT ingestion and PostgreSQL/TimescaleDB time-series storage.
- Aggregated **647 raw anomaly events into 23 actionable incidents**; evaluated day-ahead LightGBM energy forecasting over **470,857 IoT measurements** (MASE **0.842**).
- Built an Agentic RAG assistant with LangGraph (**94% HitRate@1**) and Langfuse observability, with Next.js/React and Three.js interfaces.

`FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `TimescaleDB` `MQTT` `LightGBM` `LangGraph` `Langfuse` `Next.js` `Three.js` `Docker`

**📊 Data & AI / Business Intelligence Intern** @ MyPartner | *July – August 2025*  
Developed an AI-enhanced BI system for analysis and recommendations from Paramedics ERP data.

- Cleaned and validated **22,387 records** across 20 CSV files, reducing data-quality violations from **8,497 to 36 (99.6%)**.
- Built a Streamlit/Plotly dashboard with five financial KPIs, financial health scoring, What-If simulation, and an AI assistant using Ollama/Groq.

`Python` `Pandas` `LangChain` `Ollama` `Groq` `Streamlit` `Plotly`

**🔬 Research Project — Edge AI & Anomaly Detection** @ IIT Sfax | *January – June 2025*  
Designed a system for anomaly detection, anomaly typing, and root cause analysis in multivariate IoT time series; the project led to a **submitted scientific paper**.

- Evaluated the CAT_MTL_TwoStep model on **262,800 synthetic IoT observations**: **95.0% F1** for anomaly detection, **95.7% F1** for anomaly typing, and **98.3% macro F1** for root cause analysis.
- Integrated the model into a real-time edge architecture with MQTT, FastAPI, Firebase, and Angular.

`PyTorch` `TCN` `Self-Attention` `MQTT` `FastAPI` `Firebase` `Angular` `Docker`

**💻 Full Stack Developer Intern** @ Flash Marketing Digital | *June – July 2024*
